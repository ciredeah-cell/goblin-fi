Goblin-fi

A small private tool that publishes and maintains videos on its owner's own YouTube channel.

Goblin-fi is not a product, a service, or an app you can sign up for. It is a set of scripts that run on a single machine on a home network and manage one YouTube channel — the one belonging to the person who wrote them. There is one user, and that user is the owner.

What it does

It renders and queues video content, uploads finished pieces to the owner's channel, and keeps the channel tidy afterwards: retitling, scheduling, setting visibility, and removing superseded re-renders.

Why this page exists

Google requires a homepage and a privacy policy for any application that requests access to a YouTube account, including single-user personal ones. This is that homepage. The privacy policy is here.
